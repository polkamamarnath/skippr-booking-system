# Product Thinking

## Feature 1: Real-Time Slot Availability

The most important improvement would be preventing double bookings for the same service slot. As the platform grows, multiple residents may try to book the same time slot. Real-time availability ensures accurate scheduling and improves customer trust.

## Feature 2: Service Staff Assignment & Notifications

After a booking is created, the system should automatically assign available staff and notify both the resident and the service worker. This reduces manual coordination and improves operational efficiency.

## Feature 3: Recurring Service Subscriptions

Many residents require regular services such as weekly car cleaning or monthly washroom cleaning. A recurring subscription feature would improve customer convenience and create predictable recurring revenue for the business.
